# Presentation Outline: ForestGuard AI

## Slide 1: Title
ForestGuard AI: Protecting our Forests with Edge AI.

## Slide 2: The Problem
- 15 billion trees cut down annually.
- Illegal logging accounts for up to 90% of tropical deforestation.
- Hard to monitor vast, remote areas manually.

## Slide 3: Our Solution
- Real-time monitoring using Hybrid Sensors.
- AI-driven detection (Computer Vision + Audio Analysis).
- Incident management for law enforcement.
